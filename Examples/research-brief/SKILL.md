# Research Brief

Use this skill when investigating a product, repository, technical concept, or market question.

## Workflow
1. Clarify the decision the research should support.
2. Prefer primary sources and current documentation.
3. Separate verified facts, interpretation, and unknowns.
4. Include direct source links for important claims.
5. Summarize the strongest findings before details.
6. End with practical implications and recommended next steps.

## Output format
- **Executive take:** one short paragraph.
- **Key findings:** concise bullets with source links.
- **What remains uncertain:** explicit unknowns.
- **Recommendation:** action-oriented and proportional to the evidence.
